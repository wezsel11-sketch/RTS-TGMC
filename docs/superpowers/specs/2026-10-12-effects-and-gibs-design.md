# Effects and gibs: design

Status: draft, awaiting approval. Nothing here is built yet.

## Intent

Fights in the mod are hard to read at a glance, and deaths are invisible. A marine vanishes when killed, and xenos
die with no visible cost. This work makes attacks, hits and deaths read clearly for both factions, and makes
explosions do something visible to the units they catch: a devastating blast gibs what it hits, as it does in TGMC.

## Goals

- Every marine and xeno unit has a death: a marine death sprite or fade, and a xeno death animation.
- An explosion that kills a unit can gib it, for humans and xenos alike, using TGMC's gib art (remains, gib animation,
  gib particles, blood splash).
- Attack effects for every xeno caste that is in the mod, using TGMC art only.
- Effects are built by the existing pipeline (`mod/build_sprites.py`) and checked by `mod/lint_static.py`.

## Non-goals

- No new art drawn or generated by us. Every frame comes from TGMC.
- No port of TGMC's DM code. The effects are recreated as OpenRA sequences, warheads and projectiles, using the same
  sprite sheets.
- No balance changes beyond the gib rule below. Damage and health stay as they are.
- No new abilities (for example TGMC's Behemoth earth pillar or King domination) unless a caste is added (see the
  questions below).

## Anti-goals

- An effect that hides a unit's position or health from the player.
- A gib or death that fires on a non-lethal hit.
- Effects that need a sprite the build cannot produce, or that break `mod/check_mod.sh`.

## Constraints

- TGMC art is non-commercial, and some is ShareAlike. Built output stays local under `build/`, as the README requires.
- The mod is OpenRA YAML plus Lua. Effects must fit OpenRA's sequence, warhead and projectile model at the pinned
  OpenRA version (`mod/setup.sh`).
- No audio device on this machine, so sound is checked only by its log, as it is now.

## The parts, as decided

**Gib rule (my call, mirrors TGMC).** A devastating explosion gibs a xeno unless its effective bomb armour is about
95% or more (TGMC's `XENO_EXPLOSION_GIB_THRESHOLD` is 0.95). A devastating explosion gibs a marine unless its
effective bomb armour is about 10% or more (TGMC's `HUMAN_EXPLOSION_GIB_THRESHOLD` is 0.1). The mod's marines carry
no bomb armour, so any devastating blast gibs them. Lighter explosions only damage.
The OpenRA mechanism for this is a death animation chosen by damage type. Whether the pinned OpenRA version supports
that needs checking before the plan is written.

**Gib art.** The gib animation, remains and particles come from TGMC's gib sprites (`icons/effects`, `icons/mob/xeno`,
human gib overlays). The exact sheets are to be found in the TGMC source and checked against `build_sprites.py`.

**Deaths.** Marines get a death sprite or fade (currently none). Xenos get a death animation and their remains.

**Attack effects.** Each caste in the mod gets an effect for each attack: muzzle or spit visuals, the impact and
the hit on the target, drawn from the matching TGMC `temp_visual` and caste effect sprites.

**Lint.** `lint_static.py` checks every new sequence and warhead name, as it does for the current effects.

## What's left to the builder

- Which TGMC sheet provides each effect, using the source at the TGMC commit `mod/setup.sh` pins.
- The exact frame timing and layering for each gib and death.
- Whether a gib is a sequence or a separate actor spawned on death.
- The tests: the existing `mod/test_scenarios.sh` cases, plus a scenario that kills a unit with an explosion.

## Changed while planning

The implementation plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-12-effects-and-gibs.md`) narrows two things: a gib leaves no
persistent remains and no particles, because an OpenRA death animation ends with its sequence. Warrior and Queen play
their plain death animation for an explosion kill instead of gibbing.

## Resolved (approved 2026-10-12; defaults taken by me, not stated by the human partner)

1. Castes: option (a). Effects and gibs for the xeno castes already in the mod. Adding new castes is a separate project.
2. Marine death: a fade on a normal kill, a gib when an explosion kills it.

## Open questions for the human partner (answered above)

These only the human partner can answer, and each changes what gets built.

1. **Which xeno castes?** The mod has five xeno castes (Runner, Hunter, Sentinel, Drone, Warrior) and one Queen.
   TGMC has about forty. "Do it for the other xeno castes" could mean (a) effects for the attacks of castes the mod
   already has, which is only a few new effects, or (b) adding the other castes as playable units, with their
   effects and gibs. Option (b) is a large gameplay change, not just effects. Which do you mean, and if (b), which
   castes first?
2. **Death sprite for marines.** TGMC's human sheet has only a standing state. Is a fade, a tipped-over sprite
   built from the standing frame, or a gib on every death acceptable?

The builder check reads this document against the codebase. Its open points are answered by the above or by the
calls already marked in this document.
